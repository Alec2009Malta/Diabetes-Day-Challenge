Important Note

For Parents, Teachers, and Healthcare Mentors

This game is an educational support activity for a CREST Gold project. It helps children practise general safety habits, such as telling an adult, checking glucose when advised, carrying hypo treatment, and looking after oral health.

It does not replace a child's personal diabetes care plan or advice from doctors, nurses, dietitians, dentists, or other healthcare professionals. Families should always follow their own clinical plan.
